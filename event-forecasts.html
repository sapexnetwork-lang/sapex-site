THREE LAYERS OF DETAIL

Start with the headline, drill into the rest

01

Segments

Every option or candidate tracked, ranked by projected share or chance from 0 to 100. Up to 60 can be listed.

02

Regions

The same projection by geography, so you can see where a result is being won or lost.

03

Demographics

How support splits by group for the top two or three segments. Only included when genuine polling data exists. Never guessed.

LIFE OF A FORECAST

Three states, always visible

PENDING

Being researched

A forecast request has been created and the AI is searching for real data. It stays here until research returns enough to fill it in.

PUBLISHED

Live and refreshed

Reviewed and approved by a person. From here it is refreshed in place every 24 hours and never drops off the site during an update.

INSUFFICIENT DATA

Not enough to say

After 5 research attempts with no real data, it stops retrying and is flagged for a person to look at, rather than showing made-up numbers.

OUR RULE

If we can't find real data, we say so.

Forecasts are filled in only from what the research actually finds, grounded in live web search. When something can't be sourced, such as a demographic split nobody has polled, that part is left out instead of estimated. A smaller honest forecast beats a bigger invented one.

HOW THE AI ANALYST COLLECTS DATA

How a forecast is researched

Each forecast is researched from live sources, not generated from memory.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

Live web search

Google Search grounding finds current polling, reporting and official figures for the event.

Published polls and surveys

Used for segment, regional and demographic splits. Only genuine data is used.

Administrator review

A person checks each forecast before it is published.

WHAT THE ANALYST DOES, STEP BY STEP

  1. 1

    A forecast is requested

    An administrator creates a request, which starts as pending.

  2. 2

    The AI researches it daily

    It searches for real data and returns a headline stat, a summary and ranked segments, regions and demographics.

  3. 3

    Retries if data is thin

    If research finds nothing real, it tries again on later cycles, up to 5 attempts.

  4. 4

    Flags what can't be sourced

    After 5 failed attempts, it is marked insufficient data and handed to a person instead of being filled with guesses.

  5. 5

    Refreshes once live

    Published forecasts are updated in place every 24 hours.

Sourced or left out

Any section the research can't support, such as demographic splits without polling, is left out instead of estimated.

Limitations

  • Forecasts summarise polling and reporting available at the time of the last refresh. Polls can be wrong.
  • Segment, region and demographic detail depends on what data exists and varies from event to event.
  • Forecasts are context for event risk. They are not trading signals or recommendations. See the Risk Disclosure.
