HOW IT WORKS

One question, two independent answers

A new ticket opens roughly every 24 hours, built around a real, checkable price question for example, whether a specific crypto pair will close above or below a stated level by a stated time. You cast a Yes or No vote, and immediately see the live crowd split update in real time as other traders weigh in. Separately, and without seeing how the crowd voted first, SaPEX_001.Alpha Model makes its own call on the identical question.

Free accounts see the crowd percentage update live but the AI's own call stays hidden until the ticket resolves, so you're voting on your own read of the market, not copying the model. Premium accounts unlock the AI's call before resolution, letting you compare your reasoning against the model's in real time rather than only after the fact. Every resolved ticket then rolls into the public scoreboard below not a cherry-picked highlight reel, but every ticket, win or lose.

Who it's for

Traders who want to stress-test their own market read against an AI's independent call, and anyone who enjoys a low-stakes, genuinely free way to see how their instincts stack up against both a model and a crowd of other traders.

What you get

  • A new real, verifiable price ticket roughly every 24 hours
  • Free Yes/No voting with a live, updating crowd percentage
  • A public AI Accuracy and Crowd Accuracy scoreboard, visible to everyone
  • Premium: the AI's own call unlocked before the ticket resolves

Limitations

Prediction Arena is designed as an engagement and accuracy-transparency feature, not a trading product it is not a substitute for the entry, target and stop-loss detail in the core AI Trading Signals module, and outcomes reflect a single price condition rather than a full trade plan.
