THE 24-HOUR CYCLE

One ticket. One day. One answer.

Every ticket follows the same four beats, so you always know where you are in the cycle.

01

Ticket opens

A fresh question on a real price target goes live, with the AI's call and confidence already on it.

02

You vote

Back the AI with Yes, or fade it with No. The live tally shows how the crowd is leaning.

03

News lands

If real news affects the question, an approved update is added to the ticket with its likely impact on the odds.

04

It resolves

The ticket closes against the real price and the next one opens, so there is always a fresh call to make.

ANATOMY OF A TICKET

1

The question

A plain Yes/No question about a real price target, so there is never any doubt what you are voting on.

2

The model commits to Yes or No before you vote, along with a confidence percentage.

3

The crowd tally

A running Yes/No split so you can see whether you are with the crowd, against it, or against the AI.

4

Short, sourced notes on genuine developments, each reviewed by a person before it appears.

HOW THE NEWS GETS THERE

A bot drafts. A human decides.

Updates are researched automatically but never published automatically.

Open tickets

Every open ticket is scanned several times a day. Recently checked ones are skipped.

Live web search

Draft update

Admin approval

HOW THE AI ANALYST COLLECTS DATA

Where a ticket's information comes from

Two different jobs: the AI makes its call, and a research bot watches for news that could change it.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

The ticket itself

The question, category, the AI's own Yes/No call and its confidence are stored with the ticket.

Live web search

Google Search grounding through the Gemini model finds genuine, recent news about the specific question.

Market price data

The same price data behind SaPEX NEXUS signals is what a price-target ticket resolves against.

WHAT THE ANALYST DOES, STEP BY STEP

  1. 1

    Pick open tickets

    Every open ticket is a candidate, up to about 25 per run. Tickets checked within the last day are skipped.

  2. 2

    Search for real news

    The bot searches the web for developments directly relevant to this one question, within a recent window.

  3. 3

    Decide if it matters

    It answers one thing: does this news genuinely affect whether the ticket resolves Yes or No? If not, nothing is drafted.

  4. 4

    Write the impact note

    A short headline and a brief note on how the news affects the odds of the AI's call.

  5. 5

    Hold for approval

    The note is saved as a pending draft. A person approves it before it appears.

Drafts only

The research bot never publishes, edits the AI's call or changes a vote. Its only output is a draft for human review.

Limitations

  • The Arena is for analysis and engagement. It is not a betting product and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  • The AI's confidence is a model estimate, not a probability guarantee. It will be wrong some of the time.
  • The community tally reflects the members who voted, not the market.
  • Read the full Risk Disclosure before acting on any signal.
