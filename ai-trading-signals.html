HOW IT WORKS

One engine, watching every instrument at once

SaPEX_001.Alpha Model continuously reads price action, volume and momentum across every tracked instrument rather than one chart at a time. Crypto pairs including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and XRP are re-scanned every 5 minutes; 77+ major stocks such as AAPL, TSLA, NVDA, MSFT and AMZN are re-scanned every 15. When enough of the model's criteria line up in agreement, a signal is generated and pushed straight to your dashboard no manual screening, no scrolling through a hundred charts looking for the one setup that matters right now.

This is the practical difference between a signal feed and a data feed: a data feed hands you numbers and expects you to do the interpreting. SaPEX NEXUS hands you a decision-ready call, built so that acting on it doesn't require decoding it first.

READING A SIGNAL

Four numbers, one decision

Every signal is built from the same four pieces, every time: an entry price to act on, a take-profit target that defines the win, a stop-loss level that defines the exit if the setup fails, and an AI confidence score that tells you how strongly the model's criteria agreed at scan time. Feed those four numbers straight into the built-in Position Calculator and you get a suggested position size against your real account balance and a risk percentage you choose no separate spreadsheet required.

Confidence scores are a signal of agreement, not a promise of outcome. Markets move on news that no model can price in ahead of time, which is exactly why every closed signal whether it hit target or not feeds back into your visible, per-instrument accuracy rating.

Who it's for

Active traders who want a second set of eyes across many markets at once from swing traders checking in a few times a day, to anyone glancing at the terminal between meetings who doesn't have time to scan 300+ instruments by hand.

What you get

  • Instrument, direction (BUY/SELL), entry, take-profit and stop-loss on every signal
  • An AI confidence score attached to every call
  • Historical accuracy per instrument via the Terminal Accuracy Metric Matrix
  • 5-minute refresh for crypto, 15-minute refresh for stocks
  • One-click hand-off into the built-in Position Calculator

Limitations

Signals are probabilistic, not guaranteed outcomes, and none of this is financial advice. Always size positions against your own risk tolerance. Fast-moving news can invalidate a signal before the next scan cycle completes, and thin, low-liquidity markets can slip past entry or stop levels.
