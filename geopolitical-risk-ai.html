HOW IT WORKS

Reading the news before it becomes a price move

Most trading tools start and end at the price chart. Geopolitical Risk AI starts earlier it reads global news feeds including BBC, Al Jazeera and Reuters on an ongoing basis, watching specifically for the categories of event that historically move markets in a correlated way: military conflict, economic sanctions, trade disputes, central bank decisions and regime change. Each development is scored for likely market impact and tagged by region, then surfaced as a risk ticket with a plain-language summary of what's developing and why it matters not a wall of raw headlines you have to triage yourself.

The goal is context, not noise. A single alert tells you what's forming, which markets it's likely to touch, and how severe the model thinks the impact could be giving you macro awareness before a headline gets fully priced in, rather than after the candle already moved.

SECRET INTEL · PREMIUM

A deeper layer most terminals never surface

Premium accounts unlock the Secret Intel tab, a separate panel of AI speculative intelligence built on news patterns, geopolitical theory and historical precedent. It covers scenarios standard risk feeds don't touch covert-operation-level predictions and longer-range geopolitical maneuvering rather than only reactive headline coverage.

This tab is labelled clearly for what it is: AI speculation grounded in pattern-matching, not confirmed intelligence from any government or agency. Treat it the way you'd treat any high-conviction, low-certainty forecast useful context, not a certainty to trade blindly against.

Who it's for

Traders and investors who want macro context before a headline moves their positions, not after the candle already moved and anyone who trades around global events but doesn't have hours a day to read wire coverage from multiple outlets.

What you get

  • Region-tagged risk alerts with a model-assigned severity score
  • Plain-language summaries of what's developing and why it matters
  • Coverage of military conflict, sanctions, trade disputes, and regime change
  • Roughly 15-minute refresh across tracked global news sources
  • Premium: the Secret Intel tab, with deeper predictive, speculative coverage

Limitations

Geopolitical events are inherently unpredictable, and this tool surfaces probability-weighted risk, not certainty. Secret Intel content in particular is speculative by design pattern-matched from theory and precedent, not confirmed intelligence and should inform, not replace, your own judgement.
