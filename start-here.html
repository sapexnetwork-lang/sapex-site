New to SaPEX NEXUS? Three minutes, no sign-in.

SaPEX NEXUS publishes trading signals for crypto and stocks, plus geopolitical risk alerts, an economic calendar and a conflict map. A signal is a buy or sell idea with an entry price, a target and a stop-loss. It does not place trades and does not manage money. You decide what to do with it.

READING A SIGNAL

Five numbers, in plain words

Action

BUY means the system expects the price to rise from the entry. SELL means it expects a fall.

Entry

The price at which the signal was released. Prices move, so your own fill may differ.

Target (take-profit)

The price where the signal counts as a success. It sits 3 times the asset's recent average price swing (ATR) away from the entry.

Stop (stop-loss)

The price where the signal counts as failed. It sits 1.5 times the average swing away, so the target is twice as far as the stop.

Confidence

A momentum score from 50 to 99 made by the signal engine. It is not the chance of winning. A 90 can still lose.

Expired

The signal ended without reaching the target or the stop in time. It is not counted as a win or a loss, which is why the Track Record also shows a stricter rate that treats expired signals as losses.

How often signals are produced: crypto markets are scanned about every 5 minutes and stocks about every 10 minutes, and only the strongest setup of each scan is published. A signal is not guaranteed on every scan.
