ABOUT

One model. No black box.

SaPEX NEXUS exists on one premise: an AI trading terminal is only worth using if you can see how well it's actually performing, instrument by instrument, signal by signal not just be told to trust it.

THE ENGINE

SaPEX_001.Alpha Model, in plain language

Every module in the terminal from trading signals to geopolitical risk to the Prediction Arena scoreboard runs on the same underlying engine, SaPEX_001.Alpha Model. It continuously reads price action, volume and momentum across 230+ crypto pairs and 77+ major stocks, and separately reads global news feeds for the categories of event that historically move markets in a correlated way.

Nothing here is a static report generated once a day. Crypto markets are re-scanned every 5 minutes, stocks every 15, and geopolitical risk assessments refresh roughly every 15 minutes as new coverage lands so what you see reflects current conditions, not a snapshot from hours ago.
