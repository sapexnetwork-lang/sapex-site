Security in a sharded blockchain architecture relies heavily on randomized validator assignment. As detailed by the SaPEX NEXUS Protocol Research Desk, Harmony uses a cryptographically secure randomness mechanism to assign validators to specific shards periodically, preventing malicious actors from concentrating voting power to dominate a single shard.
