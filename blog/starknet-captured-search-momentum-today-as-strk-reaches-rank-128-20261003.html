Technical Architecture and Layer 2 Mechanics

To fully grasp the fundamental value proposition behind STRK, traders must evaluate the core technical architecture of the Starknet network. Per the SaPEX NEXUS Asset Registry, Starknet operates as a Validity Rollup, commonly referred to as a zero-knowledge rollup, built directly on top of the Ethereum blockchain. The network is designed to address Ethereum's inherent scalability limitations by executing complex smart contract logic off-chain while maintaining the robust security guarantees of the Layer 1 mainnet.

The core mechanism powering Starknet relies on advanced cryptographic proofs known as STARKs, which stands for Scalable Transparent ARguments of Knowledge. According to the SaPEX NEXUS Technical Intelligence unit, Starknet bundles thousands of individual off-chain transactions into a single batch and generates a succinct STARK proof confirming their validity. This cryptographic proof is then submitted to and verified by the Ethereum mainnet in a single transaction. By verifying a single proof rather than processing every transaction individually, Starknet dramatically reduces gas costs and increases transaction throughput for end users.
