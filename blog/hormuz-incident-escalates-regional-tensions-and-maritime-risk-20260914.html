In addition to rising freight costs, maritime operators face operational delays driven by heightened security protocols. The SaPEX NEXUS Geo Risk AI model indicates a continued likelihood of localized naval patrols and heightened alert levels across the Persian Gulf over the three-month horizon. Analysis from the SaPEX NEXUS Economic Impact Model demonstrates that persistent energy cost spikes contribute directly to persistent inflationary pressure. According to long-term projections from the SaPEX NEXUS Intelligence network, prolonged regional instability accelerates corporate and sovereign initiatives to diversify energy supply chains away from single-point maritime chokepoints.
