The Situation & Verified Facts

On November 22 and 23, 2026, suspected Ukrainian drone attacks struck Russian maritime vessels in both the Mediterranean Sea off Benghazi, Libya, and international waters in the Gulf of Finland. The strikes represent a significant geographical expansion of maritime warfare targeting Russian naval and commercial logistics assets thousands of miles beyond the primary Black Sea theater of conflict. The coordinated incidents damaged multiple vessels, caused a warship to sink in North Africa, and triggered heightened security concerns across European and Mediterranean maritime trade corridors.

The Russian navy corvette Merkury capsized and sank at the Benghazi Naval Base in eastern Libya on Sunday after being struck by two explosive sea drones, injuring ten people according to Libyan military officials [2]. Hours after the corvette was struck, a second drone attack targeted a commercial vessel off the coast of Benghazi [2]. The eastern government of Libya initially reported that a suspected drone attack caused the explosion aboard the Russian vessel [1].

In a separate incident in northern Europe, the Russian cargo ship Sparta II was damaged on Saturday by a drone attack in international waters of the Gulf of Finland while traveling bound for Kaliningrad [3]. Finnish military officials stated the strike was likely carried out by Ukraine, after which the vessel drifted into Finnish territorial waters before its sixteen crew members were evacuated onto a nearby Russian vessel while coast guard vessels sprayed water cannons to extinguish container fires aboard the ship [3]. Finnish President Alexander Stubb confirmed that the vessel departed Finnish territorial waters late on Sunday after posing a serious security danger to the region [3].
