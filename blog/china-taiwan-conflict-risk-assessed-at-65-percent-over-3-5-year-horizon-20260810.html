Conversely, a limited military strike might target specific defense installations to degrade command infrastructure. While a full-scale invasion remains a tangible and high-risk scenario, intermediate measures like blockades are frequently evaluated as adaptable strategic tools.

Evaluating these distinct potential mechanics allows market participants to map out scenario-specific hedging strategies long before physical disruptions reach full intensity.

The compounding effect of supply destruction and reduced industrial output is the classic trigger for a severe global recession, characterized by declining gross domestic product across major developed and emerging economies alike.
