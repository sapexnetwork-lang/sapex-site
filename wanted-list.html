THE GATE

Four checks before anyone is published

Every profile goes through the same path, whether it came from an API or an AI search.

An official reward programme

The notice must come from a government, police force or international body. If no official bounty exists, the request ends as "no result".

At least two verified sources

Distinct domains, official or a major news agency. The page has to load and has to actually mention the person.

Amounts from the notice

FBI reward figures are taken from the FBI's own text, never from the AI.

Administrator approval

Nothing is public until a person approves it. Removing a profile takes it off the map on the next publish.

WHERE PROFILES COME FROM

Three routes in, one route out

01

FBI Wanted API

02

Regional discovery

03

HOW THE AI ANALYST COLLECTS DATA

How a profile is found, checked and approved

The AI helps find candidates and read sources. Verification rules and a human decide what is published.

WHAT THE ANALYST DOES, STEP BY STEP

  1. 1

    Find candidates

    Discovery runs about twice a day, adding only a handful of new names per run.

  2. 2

    Look each one up

    Each name is researched with official data and a web-grounded AI search.

  3. 3

    Verify every source

    A source must load, mention the person, be on a distinct domain and be official or a major news agency. Two are required.

  4. 4

    Read the reward and score it

    The amount is read from the official text, converted to a rough USD equivalent and turned into a 1 to 5 star level.

  5. 5

    Queue, approve, publish

    Profiles wait in a review queue. After approval they are published as a static file with a small photo.

The AI never has the last word

If no official bounty exists, the request ends as 'no result'. Pins are country-level and no addresses or live locations are ever stored.

Limitations

  • A reward notice is an allegation or a request for information by the issuing authority, not a finding of guilt.
  • Pins are country-level and usually mark the issuer, not where a person is. No addresses or live locations are stored.
  • Programmes change. A reward may be withdrawn, increased or closed after we last checked, so always confirm on the official notice linked on each profile.
  • The list is not complete. It only includes cases that passed verification and approval.
