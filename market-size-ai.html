MODULE 04 · MARKET SIZE

Where the capital actually is, right now.

Market Size AI renders capitalisation across the top 100 cryptocurrencies and major stock indices as a living bubble chart, sized by relative weight so shifts in where money is concentrated are visible at a glance instead of buried in a spreadsheet of numbers.

HOW IT WORKS

Capitalisation, rendered as geometry

Numbers in a table are easy to skim past a bubble that suddenly grows or shrinks relative to its neighbours is not. Market Size AI takes live capitalisation data for the top 100 cryptocurrencies and major stock indices and renders it as a bubble chart, with each bubble sized by that asset's weight relative to everything else in view. A single toggle switches the whole map between the Crypto Universe and the Stock Universe, so you're always comparing like with like.

The practical effect is speed: dominance shifts and sector rotation and away from certain categories become visible the moment they happen, rather than requiring you to notice a percentage change buried three columns deep in a data table.

Market Size AI is deliberately a snapshot-and-trend view. Paired with the AI Trading Signals module, a large, growing bubble alongside a fresh BUY signal on the same asset tells a different story than the same signal appearing against a shrinking one use this to see where attention is moving, then let Signals confirm whether it's actionable.

Who it's for

Traders who want a fast visual read on capital flow before drilling into individual instruments, and anyone trying to spot sector rotation across crypto or equities without cross-referencing a dozen separate market-cap rankings.

What you get

  • Relative market-cap sizing across the top 100 cryptocurrencies
  • A separate bubble map for major stock indices, one toggle away
  • Category and sector grouping within each universe
  • Movement trends over time as capitalisation shifts

Limitations

This is a snapshot view, not a trading signal on its own. It's most useful read alongside the Signals module, and relative bubble sizing can shift quickly during periods of extreme volatility in any single large-cap asset.
