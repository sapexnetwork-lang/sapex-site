HOW THE AI ANALYST COLLECTS DATA

Where the calendar comes from

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

Published release calendars

Scheduled releases and central-bank meetings come from the publishers' own calendars.

AI impact estimate

The AI rates each event's likely market impact as high, medium or low.

WHAT THE ANALYST DOES, STEP BY STEP

  1. 1

    Collect the schedule

    Upcoming releases and meetings are gathered for the coming days.

  2. 2

    Classify each event

    Events are grouped by type, such as rate decision, inflation or jobs.

  3. 3

    Estimate impact

    The AI rates how large a market reaction each is likely to cause.

  4. 4

    Rank and display

    Events are shown by day with high-impact ones standing out.

Impact, not direction

The AI estimates how big a move might be, not whether prices will rise or fall.

Limitations

  • Impact rankings are the AI's prediction of volatility, not of direction.
  • Release times can change. Always confirm with the official publisher before trading around an event.
  • The calendar is not a trading signal. See the Risk Disclosure.
